# Key Insights – AI Wellness Companion

## Survey Overview

The Wellness Companion survey contains 35 responses. The survey was used to understand stress patterns, major mental-wellness factors, interest in an AI wellness assistant, preferred features, trust in AI suggestions, and preferred communication tone.

## 1. Age Group Distribution

- 18–22: 27 respondents
- 23–30: 3 respondents
- 31–40: 3 respondents
- Above 40: 2 respondents

The 18–22 age group represents the majority of the survey responses.

## 2. Stress Frequency

- Sometimes: 17 respondents
- Rarely: 7 respondents
- Daily: 6 respondents
- Weekly: 5 respondents

"Sometimes" was the most common response for stress frequency.

## 3. Main Factors Affecting Mental Wellness

- Studies: 12 respondents
- Financial stress: 7 respondents
- Sleep problems: 6 respondents
- Work pressures: 6 respondents
- Social issues: 4 respondents

Studies were the most frequently selected factor affecting mental wellness in this survey.

## 4. Interest in an AI Wellness Assistant

- Maybe: 19 respondents
- Yes: 12 respondents
- No: 4 respondents

Most respondents selected either "Maybe" or "Yes", indicating interest or openness toward an AI wellness assistant among the surveyed participants.

## 5. Most Helpful Features

- Stress relief exercises: 13 respondents
- Mood tracking: 8 respondents
- Sleep improvement tips: 7 respondents
- Meditation reminders: 7 respondents

Stress relief exercises were the most selected feature.

## 6. Trust in AI Wellness Suggestions

- Yes: 17 respondents
- Somewhat: 15 respondents
- No: 3 respondents

Most respondents showed either complete or partial trust in AI-generated wellness suggestions.

## 7. Preferred Assistant Tone

- Friendly: 21 respondents
- Calm: 6 respondents
- Motivational: 4 respondents
- Professional: 4 respondents

A friendly communication style was the most preferred tone.

## Overall Observations

The survey indicates that stress is present at different frequencies among the respondents, with studies being the most commonly reported wellness factor.

Respondents showed interest or openness toward an AI wellness assistant, while stress relief exercises were the most preferred feature.

The results also show that a friendly communication style was strongly preferred and that most respondents expressed at least some level of trust in AI wellness suggestions.

## Data Analyst Perspective

The survey data can help guide the design of an AI wellness companion by identifying:

- Common wellness concerns
- User stress patterns
- Preferred assistant features
- User trust levels
- Preferred communication style

These findings were used as part of the survey-driven design of the Wellness Companion project.
